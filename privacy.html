Privacy Policy – Genius History Uploader

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Genius History Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "Genius History Official" to upload and schedule that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), the app uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos to the owner's own channel;
  • set and update the video title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions, playlist, audience ("made for kids") setting and publish schedule;
  • read the owner's own channel, playlists and recent videos (titles, view counts, publish dates) to choose a playlist and related-video links.

The app does not access any other person's account or data.

How data is stored

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer and is never shared. Upload logs (date, video title, video link) are kept on the owner's computer and in the owner's own Google Sheet.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any data with anyone. No advertising or analytics services are used.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account security settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access stops the app immediately.
