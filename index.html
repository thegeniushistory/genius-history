Genius History Uploader

A private tool for the YouTube channel "Genius History Official"

Genius History Uploader is a small personal tool used only by the owner of the "Genius History Official" YouTube channel. It uploads the channel owner's own videos to their own channel and sets the title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions, playlist, audience setting and publish schedule.

It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account. It uses the official YouTube Data API with the channel owner's permission.
